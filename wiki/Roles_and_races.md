# Roles and races

[Home](Home.md) | [Differences from the source variants](Variant_differences.md)

Besides NetHack's 13 roles and 5 races there are 8 more roles and 9 more
races. All but the Cartographer and the ghost come from other variants,
and they play as they do there: nethackwiki's pages for those variants
apply, and [Differences from the source variants](Variant_differences.md)
lists where this build departs from them. Every role has its own quest
(behind the portals on its ring, like every quest here), quest artifact,
sacrifice gifts, starting pet, skills, intrinsics and
[techniques](Techniques.md).

## Roles

### Necromancer

Slash'EM's, as Hack'EM has it; chaotic. Starts with the spells summon
undead, command undead and drain life, wands of draining and fear, and a
pick-axe for digging up graves. Undead are sometimes peaceful to a
necromancer and are the only creatures one can tame, and `#chat` charms
zombies with an ancient chant. Necromancers see whether things are blessed
or cursed, resist level drain and sickness, and are warned of undead.
Quest: recover the Great Dagger of Glaurgnaa from the demon Maugneshaagar
for the Dark Lord. Gift: Serpent's Tongue, a poisonous dagger.

### Flame Mage

Slash'EM's, as Hack'EM has it; lawful or neutral. Fire resistant, which
also protects their belongings. Casts fire bolt, fireball and flame sphere
more easily, and starts with a wand of fire and a fire bomb. From XL 5 on
they are vulnerable to cold, and they can't learn cold spells. Quest:
retrieve the Candle of Eternal Flame from the Water Mage for the High Flame
Mage. Gift: Firewall, a burning staff.

### Ice Mage

Slash'EM's, as Hack'EM has it; neutral or chaotic. The cold counterpart:
cold resistant, casts freeze sphere and cone of cold, reads scrolls of
ice, never slips on ice and walks on water from XL 15. Vulnerable to fire
from XL 5. Quest: take the Storm Whistle back from Ragnaros, lord of fire
elementals, for the High Ice Mage. Gift: Deep Freeze, an icy staff.

### Jedi

SlashTHEM's, as Hack'EM has it; lawful. A warrior of the Force armed with
a lightsaber, which has to be applied to switch it on and only cuts while
lit. Lit, it lights the area and uses up its charge. A scroll of charging
refills it, and from XL 5 so does the charge saber technique, which pours
the Jedi's own energy into it. It melts locks and cuts through doors
(`#force`). A skilled Jedi deflects missiles, cuts foes' weapons in half,
and can throw the lit lightsaber and call it back with the Force. Jedi
fight in robes, not armor, and must not attack the peaceful. Quest: find
the Lightsaber Prototype in the Outer Rim before Lord Sidious does. Gift:
the cloak Deluder.

### Pirate

From SLASH'EM Extended and SlashTHEM, as in SpliceHack and Hack'EM;
neutral or chaotic; human, gnome, orc, illithid, tortle, vampire, werewolf
or ghost. Starts with a cutlass (scimitar), a flintlock and bullets,
knives, a leather jacket, high boots, rum, sea biscuits, bananas, an
oilskin sack and a ring, has a parrot or a monkey for a pet, and can swim.
Pirates hear every message in pirate speech and know many things by
pirate names (rum, cutlass, sea biscuit, ditty bag, coffer, eye-patch;
potions are bottles). They find more artifacts, and rise as skeletal
pirates when they die.

Firearms are a new skill. A flintlock fires one bullet per turn at up to
eight squares, and the bullets are used up. It misfires now and then and
can jam, from curses, rust, clumsiness, bad luck or lack of skill; grease
wears off first, and blessed guns resist. Grease, a greased towel, a dip
in oil or enchant weapon unjams it. A cursed gun loaded with cursed
bullets blows up. Monsters use flintlocks the same way.

The only sacrifice gift is the Marauder's Map: read it to map your
surroundings, as often as you like, and invoke it to detect objects.
Crowning makes you the Pirate King (or Queen), with Reaver, the cutlass
that steals. The quest (Tortuga, Shipwreck Island, Blackbeard's Ghost) is
for the Treasury of Proteus, a sea chest that absorbs curses, polymorphs
what you keep in it, and brings undead pirates after you.

### Convict

The Convict patch, as in EvilHack and Hack'EM; always chaotic, and open
to any race the table below allows. Starts hungry and punished with a
heavy iron ball and chain, in a cursed striped shirt, with a spoon, some
rocks and a sewer rat for a pet.

Shopkeepers won't let anyone wearing a visible striped prison shirt into
their shops, and the town watch and vault guards know a convict's face
from the wanted posters, unless the convict is polymorphed or has covered
their face with a towel or blindfold. Domestic animals are wary of
convicts, but rats can be soothed by chittering (`#chat`). Convicts
stomach rotten and nasty food better, last twice as long once hungry,
resist sickness (and later poison), feel no guilt over robbing shops or
killing guards, and make sneak attacks with a spoon on fleeing or helpless
foes.

Their gods are Faerunian (Ilmater, Grumbar, Tymora), and their sacrifice
gifts include the Luck Blade. The quest (Castle Waterdeep Dungeon, the
Warden's Level, Warden Arianna) is for the Iron Spoon of Liberation,
chosen over SLASH'EM's Iron Ball and EvilHack's Striped Shirt as Hack'EM
chose it. It digs like a pick-axe, engraves like an athame, frees you from
a ball and chain, and can be invoked to walk through walls.

### Infidel

By Tomsod, from EvilHack; human, elf, orc, giant, centaur, illithid,
draugr or vampire. A cultist of Moloch: unaligned, with a random role's
pantheon as the three gods of heaven. The Cult entrusts the Infidel with
the Amulet of Yendor from the start. Moloch demands regular sacrifices and
hears prayer outside Gehennom only now and then, or always while you carry
the Idol of Moloch. Infidels shrug off curses, are hated by lawful angels
and liked by demons, and a crowned Infidel becomes a demon with wings
(folded under hard body armor) and a barbed tail. The quest (the Hidden
Temple, the Howling Forest, the Paladin) is for the Idol of Moloch.

Infidels win differently: see [the Infidel](Variant_differences.md#infidel).

### Cartographer

New in this build: a mapmaker and explorer for a world of compass, rings
and portals. See [Cartographer](Cartographer.md).

## Races

| race | alignments | roles |
|---|---|---|
| giant | any | Barbarian, Caveman, Monk, Priest, Samurai, Valkyrie, Wizard, Convict, Flame Mage, Infidel, Necromancer |
| centaur | neutral, chaotic | Barbarian, Healer, Monk, Priest, Ranger, Valkyrie, Cartographer, Convict, Ice Mage, Infidel, Necromancer |
| illithid | chaotic | Priest, Wizard, Convict, Ice Mage, Infidel, Necromancer, Pirate |
| tortle | lawful, neutral | Archeologist, Barbarian, Healer, Monk, Priest, Samurai, Tourist, Wizard, Cartographer, Jedi, Pirate |
| draugr | chaotic | Barbarian, Rogue, Convict, Ice Mage, Infidel, Necromancer |
| vampire | neutral, chaotic | Archeologist, Barbarian, Rogue, Wizard, Convict, Ice Mage, Infidel, Necromancer, Pirate |
| werewolf | chaotic | Barbarian, Rogue, Ranger, Convict, Pirate |
| doppelganger | neutral, chaotic | Healer, Monk, Priest, Rogue, Wizard, Convict |
| ghost | any | Archeologist, Priest, Rogue, Samurai, Tourist, Wizard, Cartographer, Convict, Necromancer, Pirate |

The giant, centaur, illithid, tortle and draugr come from EvilHack; the
vampire from EvilHack, Hack'EM and Slash'EM; the werewolf and doppelganger
from Slash'EM. The ghost is new.

* **Giant**: huge, strong and slow. Carries more, and carries, throws and
  steps onto boulders. Can't wear body armor or cloaks, ride, or be
  stealthy. Digs fast, kicks doors open and shatters weapons.
* **Centaur**: fast (speed 18) and a strong kicker. No boots and no
  riding. Gets extra shots with bows and crossbows, and uses lances and
  polearms as if mounted.
* **Illithid**: a mind flayer, telepathic and psychic resistant, with a
  brain-eating tentacle attack, a psychic blast (`#monster`) and the
  psionic wave spell that only illithids can learn. A heavy metal helmet
  blocks its psionics. Flies from XL 12.
* **Tortle**: a turtle folk that hides in its shell (`#monster`), swims and
  breathes water. Natural armor that improves with level, but no body
  armor or boots.
* **Draugr**: an undead Norse warrior that eats only rotten meat, returns
  from death a few times, and bites to zombify. Fire does it extra harm,
  and shopkeepers, the watch and lawful or neutral priests shun it.
* **Vampire**: lives on blood (drains fresh corpses, drinks potions of
  blood, feeds by biting) and shapechanges into a bat, fog cloud or warg
  (`#monster`) from XL 3. Hurt by silver, fire and holy water. Flies from
  XL 12 and regenerates from XL 20.
* **Werewolf**: born a lycanthrope, which can't be cured. Changes into its
  wolf form at will (`#youpoly`) from XL 3, and fights with berserk fury.
* **Doppelganger**: polymorphs at will (`#youpoly`), remembering the forms
  it has eaten, and gains polymorph control at XL 9.
* **Ghost**: drifts through walls at the cost of power, is hard to hurt
  with ordinary blows, and is clumsy with material things. See
  [Ghost](Ghost_race.md).

The undead races (draugr, vampire, ghost) are harmed by holy water and
blessed weapons, are healed rather than killed by death rays, and are
often left alone by ordinary undead. Lawful gods smite the prayers of a
draugr or vampire, and neutral ones now and then (outside Gehennom). A
ghost is only a restless spirit, and may pray to any god.
