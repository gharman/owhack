## Powers

* **Wielded: fast**, like wearing speed boots.
* **Invoked: a controlled level teleport** (*Pathfinder points the way.*),
  as if you had teleport control, whether or not you do. In the open
  world a level teleport takes you to another ring: name the depth you
  want and you arrive on that ring. The usual limits apply:
  * not while carrying the Amulet of Yendor, nor in Sokoban ("You feel
    very disoriented for a moment.");
  * not past the Barrier into Gehennom until you have entered Gehennom
    the proper way (you are stopped at the ring before it);
  * not out of One-eyed Sam's Market, which forbids level teleporting.
  The usual invoke timeout applies.

## Strategy

The open world's rings are wide and far apart: a trip from the plaza to
depth 20 is several hundred squares. Pathfinder turns that into one move,
and the Cartographer's *waymark* technique brings you back to the place
you marked. Together they make the Cartographer the fastest traveller in
the game (short of the Amulet, which stops both).
