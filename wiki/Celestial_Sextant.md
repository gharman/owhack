# The Celestial Sextant

[Home](Home.md) · [Cartographer](Cartographer.md) · [Quest](Cartographer_quest.md)

> *"As you lift the Celestial Sextant, the Labyrinth shudders around you.
> For a moment you can feel every road in the world, taut and true, like
> the lines of a well-drawn chart."*

The **Celestial Sextant** is the [Cartographer](Cartographer.md)'s quest
artifact: the instrument with which the first of the Society of
Cartographers took the sightings that fixed every road of the world in
its place. Asterion, the Minotaur of the Labyrinth, has stolen it; it lies
beside him at the heart of the Labyrinth.

## Powers

* **Carried: magic resistance.**
* **Carried: portal sense.** Each time you move, magic portals within the
  surrounding area (the same ~120 × 60 squares that "whole level" effects
  cover in the open world) that you haven't already got on your map are
  marked on it: *You sense the pull of a magic portal.*
* **Applied: a perfect sighting.** It works as a [sextant](Sextant.md)
  that is never off, and it needs no sky. It works while you are blind,
  under a roof, under water, engulfed, or underground (*The Celestial
  Sextant sights stars that you cannot see.*). In a branch it tells you
  your level and depth, and also where in the world that branch's way out
  lies (its ring, and the centre's distance and bearing from there) and
  the nearest portals to it.
* **Invoked: charting.** Magic-maps the neighbourhood: in the open world
  the ~120 × 60 area around you, in a branch the whole level. In the open
  world it then marks **every portal of your ring and of the rings on
  either side**, even those on land that hasn't been made yet (they will
  be there when it is). It fails, confusing you, on levels that can't be
  magic-mapped. The usual invoke timeout applies.

## Strategy

Invoke it when you arrive on a new ring to put all its portals, and the
neighbouring rings' portals, on your map; then `_` travel to the one you
want. Its magic resistance is the Cartographer's best source of it.
